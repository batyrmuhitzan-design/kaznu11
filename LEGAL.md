## 2. Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

### Data we store
- **On-device only**: login session, student ID, display name, theme/language, read marks,
  notification preferences, locally cached schedule/news/grades.
- **System permissions used**: Notifications (local reminders & Live Activity), optional Location
  (only to detect "near campus" for campus services), optional Haptics.
- Live Activities and notifications are scheduled **locally on your iPhone**; no cloud push unless
  a future server feature is enabled explicitly.

### Sharing
- We do not sell or rent personal data.
- When you intentionally use the iOS Share Sheet (AirDrop, Telegram, WeChat, printers, "Save to
  Files"), the receiving third party's own privacy policy applies.
- If the app later connects to Univer/Kaspi/etc. with your credentials, those services' policies
  apply to their systems.

### Control
- You can revoke any permission any time: iOS Settings → KazNU Helper, or in-app
  (Settings → notification switches).
- Removing the app deletes all locally stored data.

中文：本应用全部个人数据（会话、学号、偏好、缓存课表等）仅保存在你的设备上，不出售、不共享。
通知与灵动岛均在本地调度。当你主动通过系统分享给微信/Telegram/打印机/文件等第三方时，适用
对方自身的隐私政策。你随时可在系统设置或应用内关闭权限；卸载应用即删除本地数据。

## 5. Notification Categories & Defaults

| Category | Default | Can user disable |
|---|---|---|
| Class reminders (T-60 / T-30 Live Activity / T-0) | **On** | Yes (Settings → 上课与成绩提醒) |
| Grade / course-change alerts (future server push) | **On** | Yes |
| News & campus announcements | **On** | Yes (Settings → News notifications) |

News banners are only produced when the "News notifications" switch is enabled.
